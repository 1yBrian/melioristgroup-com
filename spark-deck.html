How it was made, honestly. Seventy-two candidate cards were drafted against the Improbability Drive — an engine that braids oracle readings, live encyclopedia pulls, and weighted wholesomeness into every prompt — then a panel of adversarial judges cut twenty-five for being stock icebreakers, therapy-speak, or stating shaky science as fact. The fifty-two that survived earn their place. The cuts are kept; the cutting room is where the craft lives.

Two standards govern every card: nothing a generic workbook could have written, and nothing stated as fact that wasn't checked.

Who is this for?

Trainers, facilitators, coaches, and educators who hold real rooms — including rooms with neurodivergent participants. Several cards are built precisely for the people the standard icebreaker leaves out.

What does "customizable" mean?

The deck is a plain editable file plus an app that reads it. Rewrite any card in your voice, retire what your rooms reject, add what they ask for. Your Edition takes it to paper: we coordinate the print run, your name goes on the box.

What's the Improbability Drive?

The anti-predictability engine behind the deck — it judges its own output and warps anything a standard assistant could have written. It ships with every purchase under an MIT license. Cards you generate with it are yours entirely.

What can I do with the cards?

Run them in your practice forever, customize freely, print for your own tables in any quantity. The plain-language practitioner license fits on one page, and the honest answer to edge cases is usually yes.
