Why this exists.

While designing this site, Brian kept running into the same wall: the AI kept predicting the most probable next sentence — the same "Status Foe" language every other site uses. Best practices. Proven frameworks. The gravity of the common way.

So he built a countermeasure. The i-Drive braids your question with entropy — old wisdom, true absurdities, quiet wonder — and answers from somewhere the question alone would never reach. Douglas Adams powered a starship this way. We run it a little smaller.

You ask: "How do I get more clients?"

Pick your three favorite past clients and make them embarrassingly successful — in public, with your fingerprints visible. Document what you removed from their situation, not what you added. New clients orbit results that move.
