A message from Brian

This isn't a form. It's a calibration — the better version of your work is already in you, and these questions are how we clear the clutter around it.

Every question tells you why I'm asking. Everything you write stays in your words, and your words are what I work from. Your answers save as you go — nothing you write can be lost.

What it captures

  • Your values — tapped first, starred second, defined in your own terms
  • Where you most want to invest your attention
  • Your strengths at their best, and a collaboration that moved you
  • A win you'd actually believe — the Tuesday version
  • Your most important project, right now
  • Your why — written in clay, reshapeable as you grow
  • Your escape routes, and how you'd want to be invited back

What happens with it

Your words come straight to Brian — and back to you: a copy in your inbox if you choose, and a personal page where your why stays visible, your next step stays close, and a direct line to Brian stays open.

Privacy, by architecture: your page opens only with a personal key that comes from Brian. No account, no password, nothing to remember.
